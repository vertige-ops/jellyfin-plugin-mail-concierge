# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (summary)

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text there is authoritative; this is a short summary.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, respectful and patient.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, or temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
